# Setu v1 Known Risks Register

**Status: prototype.** Setu is an unaudited Stellar **testnet** prototype (`not production-secure`). This register catalogs known cryptographic, circuit, contract, privacy, and operational risks in the current build and links each risk to its tracking issue or mitigation. For narrative caveats, see [docs/privacy-compliance-limitations.md](./privacy-compliance-limitations.md).

---

## Known Risks Table

| **R-01** | **Prover-asserted `recipientId` and `purpose` in disclosure receipts** (`circuits/disclosure.circom` hashes them into `discloseHash`, but they are not bound into `commitment` at deposit time) | Circuit / Soundness | High | **Open limitation.** Documented in `disclosure.circom` and `privacy-compliance-limitations.md`; future circuit revision will bind recipient/purpose at deposit or verify an off-ramp attestation. | `#1`, `#7` |

| **R-03** | **Legacy `associationRoot == 0` bypass branch in `circuits/main.circom`** (circuit skips Merkle inclusion check when `associationRoot == 0`) | Circuit | Medium | **Mitigated on-chain.** `PrivacyPoolsContract::withdraw` panics unless `has_association_set()` is `true` (non-zero root). | **R-04** | **Prover reuse of `receiptNonce` re-introduces `auditorTag` linkability** (`auditorTag = Poseidon255(viewingKey, nullifierHash, receiptNonce)`) | Privacy | Medium | **Mitigated in v2 when honest.** v2 adds `receiptNonce` so distinct nonces yield unlinkable tags, but the circuit cannot enforce nonce freshness against a self-sabotaging prover. | `#3`, `#21` |
| **R-05** | **On-chain `verify_disclosure` publishes `nullifierHash <-> commitment` link** to all ledger observers | Privacy | High (if used for private audit) | **By design for public verification.** Docstrings and docs warn single-regulator workflows to verify receipts off-chain (`auditor_recompute.circom` + `snarkjs`) instead of calling `verify_disclosure` on-chain. | `#7`, `#55` |
| **R-06** | **No relayer in v1 — withdrawer signs and pays Soroban tx fees directly**, leaking sender account and timing metadata in the Stellar envelope | Privacy / Network | High | **Design-only today.** Relayer architecture and threat trade-offs are specified in `docs/relayer-withdrawal-design.md` and `docs/relayer-withdrawal-privacy.md`. | `#6`, `#53` |
| **R-07** | **Centralized `admin` privileges** (`admin` can rotate `associationRoot` via `set_association_root` and `dvk` via `set_disclosure_vk` at any time) | Contract Governance | Medium | **Partially mitigated.** `set_disclosure_vk` emits a `("dvk", "set")` event with the SHA-256 key commitment (`docs/vk-commitments.md`), making key rotation publicly auditable. | `#5`, `#61` |
